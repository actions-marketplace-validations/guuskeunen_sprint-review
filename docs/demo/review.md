# Sprint review November 16, 2026

_November 3 to November 16, 2026 · Northside Cycles_

**The front desk sees a whole customer on one page**

## Customer profile

Front desk staff see everything a customer bought or had repaired on one page.

- Purchases, repairs and services sit in one list with their source
- Every bike has its own card with the next service

## Repair ticket scanning

A photo of a handwritten repair ticket becomes a repair, with no retyping.

- Fields that are hard to read are flagged

## Workshop planner

The planner shows at a glance how many jobs are done and where work is stuck.

- A bar shows how much of today is done
- Status counts load with less work

## Duplicate customers

Merged customers stop coming back as duplicates.

- A merged customer no longer shows up in duplicate alerts

## Look and feel

Labels look the same everywhere, and tables work on a phone.

- Each source has a fixed color
- Tables on a phone scroll sideways

## What it looks like

- **Workshop planner**: Look at the progress bar and the counts per status.
- **Repairs (phone)**: The table now scrolls sideways instead of squeezing its columns.
