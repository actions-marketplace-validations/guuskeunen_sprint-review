This sprint focused on text. You can now leave a text edit, come back and carry on, and you can set wrap width right from the editor. We also added a minimap, made frames smarter, and improved flowchart navigation.

Text editing Faster work

Users keep typing in a text after switching to another window and back.

  • Edit continues when the window loses focus, and ends only on a click elsewhere
  • The open text updates with changes from another tab or a collaborator
  • Ctrl/Cmd plus, minus and 0 zoom around the caret while editing
Demo: start editing a text, switch to another app, come back and keep typing. Then change the same text in a second tab and return to show the update. Finish with keyboard zoom while the caret is in the text.

Text wrapping Faster work

Users set a text's wrap width by dragging its side while editing.

  • Alt+W turns text auto-resize on or off; zoomed-out text wraps wider
  • Clicking the auto-resize toggle no longer ends the edit
  • A rotated text stays in place when auto-resize is switched on or off
Demo: edit a free text, drag its left or right side to wrap it, then press Alt+W. Mention that the resize is part of the single undo step. Rotate a text and toggle auto-resize to show it stays put.

Minimap Faster work

Users see their whole drawing in a minimap and jump around it.

  • Toggle the minimap from the footer, context menu, command palette or Preferences
  • Drag the viewport on the minimap, or click outside it to center
  • With the minimap toggled off, zoom buttons replace the zoom menu
Demo: open a large drawing, turn on the minimap bottom-left and drag the viewport. Say it is not available on phones. The zoom buttons point only matters for editors that hide the minimap.
